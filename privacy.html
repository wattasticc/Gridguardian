Grid Guardian Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 26, 2026

1. Introduction

Grid Guardian is a Discord community bot designed to provide moderation, leveling, roles, notifications, giveaways, economy, and other community features.

This Privacy Policy explains what information Grid Guardian may collect, how that information is used, and how it is protected.

2. Information We Collect

Grid Guardian may store information necessary for the bot's features to function. This may include Discord user IDs, Discord server IDs, channel IDs, role IDs, moderation records, leveling information, economy information, and bot configuration settings.

When users choose to connect supported social media accounts, Grid Guardian may also receive information provided through the applicable platform's authorization system.

3. How We Use Information

Information is used only to provide and operate Grid Guardian's features, including moderation, leveling, server configuration, notifications, giveaways, economy systems, and connected social-media features.

4. Social Media Connections

If a user chooses to connect a social media account to Grid Guardian, the bot may access information permitted by the authorization scopes approved by the user and the applicable platform.

Grid Guardian does not request access to information that is not necessary for the connected feature.

5. Information Sharing

Grid Guardian does not sell personal information.

Information may be processed through third-party services when necessary to provide specific features, such as Discord or supported social-media APIs.

6. Data Storage and Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect stored information from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

No online service can guarantee complete security of information, but Grid Guardian is designed to limit stored information to what is reasonably necessary for its features.

7. Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as it is necessary to provide Grid Guardian's features or maintain server configuration and moderation records.

8. Data Deletion

Users or server administrators who want information associated with Grid Guardian removed may contact the Grid Guardian administrator with a request.

9. Children's Privacy

Grid Guardian is intended to provide general Discord community functionality and does not intentionally collect personal information beyond what is necessary for its features.

10. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Grid Guardian's features or data practices change. The updated policy will be posted on this page.

11. Contact

For privacy questions or data-related requests, contact the Grid Guardian administrator through the official Grid Guardian support channels.
